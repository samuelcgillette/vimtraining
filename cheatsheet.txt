================================================================================
                        VIM MOVEMENT CHEAT SHEET
================================================================================

  All movements are typed in Normal mode. Press Esc first if unsure.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHARACTERS AND LINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

          k              h    left
          ^              j    down
     h <     > l         k    up
          v              l    right
          j


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LINE ENDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  0    start of the line
  $    end of the line

       The Emperor Protects.
       ^                   ^
       0                   $


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILE ENDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  gg   top of the file
  G    bottom of the file


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  w    next word
  e    end of word
  b    back a word

       Only in death does duty end
            ^  ^   ^ ^
            b  |   e w
               cursor

================================================================================
