  This is one way to complete each erratum. There are others, and any
  of them is fine if the diff check comes back clean. The errata can
  be done in any sequence.

  The fully corrected file is keys/02-guardsmen-primer-solved.txt


  ERRATUM 1  -  Spell the weapon right                     (Lesson 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :%s/lasgnu/lasgun/g
    This fixes all four, in SECTIONS 1, 2 and 4.

  ERRATUM 2  -  Better treatment, many shots         (Lessons 4, 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "Treat it worse..." line, type :s/worse/better/
    On the power pack line, put the cursor on the "e" of "exactly" and
    press d2w to delete "exactly three ". Press i, type "many " (with
    the space) and press Esc.

  ERRATUM 3  -  No licking                                  (Lesson 4)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "...if you must. Or by licking it." line, put the cursor on
    the space after "must." and press d$.

  ERRATUM 4  -  Untick the boxes, remove the copy     (Lessons 3, 10)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Put the cursor on the first "x" inside [x]. Press Ctrl-v, then
    jjjj to cover all five boxes. Press r then Space.
    Move to either "Recite the Litany of Accuracy." line and press dd.

  ERRATUM 5  -  Uppercase the heading                       (Lesson 9)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move to "section 3: know your enemy", press V, then U.

  ERRATUM 6  -  The Ork, corrected                    (Lessons 1, 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the Ork line:
      :s/small/big/
      :s/quiet/loud/
    Then put the cursor on the first "o" in "Shoooooot" and press 4x.

  ERRATUM 7  -  The Tyranid's real goal              (Lesson 12)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the Tyranid line, put the cursor on the "b" in "be your friend"
    and press dt. to delete up to the period. Press i, type "eat you"
    and press Esc.

  ERRATUM 8  -  "where", twice                             (Lesson 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the Aeldari line: :s/wehre/where/g
    The g at the end catches both on the same line.

  ERRATUM 9  -  The missing line                            (Lesson 6)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    With the cursor on the Aeldari line, press o. Type
    "The heretic looks just like you. Stay alert." and press Esc.

  ERRATUM 10  -  SECTION 4                    (Lessons 5, 8, 11, 12)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    "Commisar": put the cursor on its "s" (fs gets you there from the
    start of the line), press a, type s, press Esc.

    "rihgt": put the cursor on the "h" and press x then p. x cuts the
    "h" and p pastes it back one character later. "xp" is the classic
    Vim trick for swapping two letters, just like dd then p swaps two
    lines.

    "fine, honestly.": from the start of the line press ff, then ; to
    land on the "f" in "fine". Press d$, then A, type "a worse one."
    and press Esc.


  CHECK YOUR WORK
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :w
    :!diff % %:h/keys/02-guardsmen-primer-solved.txt

THE IMPERIAL INFANTRYMAN'S UPLIFTING PRIMER
Field Edition, Chapter Three: Your Lasgun

SECTION 1: KNOW YOUR WEAPON

Your lasgun is your best friend. Treat it better than you treat yourself.
A standard power pack holds enough charge for many shots.
It can be recharged by leaving it in a fire, if you must.
Guardsmen call the lasgun a "flashlight". Do not let the nickname fool you.

SECTION 2: THE DAILY RITES OF MAINTENANCE

[ ] Anoint the barrel with sacred oil.
[ ] Recite the Litany of Accuracy.
[ ] Check the power pack for cracks.
[ ] Thank the machine spirit of your lasgun.

SECTION 3: KNOW YOUR ENEMY

The Ork is big, green, and very loud. Shoot it many times.
The Tyranid wants to eat you. Do not let it.
The Aeldari are fast. Aim where they will be, not where they are.
The heretic looks just like you. Stay alert.

SECTION 4: REMEMBER

Your Commissar is your friend. Your Commissar is always right.
Losing your lasgun is a crime. Running from the enemy is a worse one.
