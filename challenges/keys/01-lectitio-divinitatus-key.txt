================================================================================
  KEY:  CHALLENGE 1  -  THE DEFILED SCRIPTURE
================================================================================

  This is one way to complete each order. There are others, and any
  of them is fine if the diff check comes back clean. The orders can be
  done in any sequence.

  The fully restored file is keys/01-lectitio-divinitatus-solved.txt


  ORDER 1  -  Delete the cultists' lines                    (Lesson 3)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move to "Blood for the Blood God!" and press dd.
    Move to "The Golden Throne is empty..." and press 2dd to delete it
    and "Give yourself to the warp." together.
    (V then j then d does the same thing.)

  ORDER 2  -  Restore the Emperor's name                    (Lesson 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :%s/Warmaster/Emperor/g
    This fixes all five at once: one on PAGE SEVEN, one on PAGE EIGHT
    and three in the litany.

  ORDER 3  -  Scrub the eight-pointed stars           (Lessons 7, 11)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "Wh8re His light falls..." line, press 0 then f8 to land
    on the first 8 and press re. Then press ; to jump to the next 8
    and press re again.

  ORDER 4  -  Delete the mocking aside                     (Lesson 12)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "...so that we may live (for now, heh heh)." line, put the
    cursor on the space before "(" and press dt. to delete up to the
    period.

  ORDER 5  -  Restore the bullet points                    (Lesson 10)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Put the cursor on the "P" of "Pray at dawn". Press Ctrl-v, then
    jjj to cover all four duties. Press I, type "- " (dash and space),
    and press Esc.

  ORDER 6  -  Restore the fourth duty                   (Lessons 5, 12)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "Never read a forbidden book..." line, press f, to jump to
    the comma, then d$ to delete to the end of the line.
    Press A, type ", so that your soul stays pure." and press Esc.

  ORDER 7  -  Redact the daemon's name                      (Lesson 9)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Put the cursor on the "K" in "Ka'Bandha". Press v, then t and
    Space to select everything up to (not including) the space after
    the name. Press rX.
    Result: XXXXXXXXX (nine X's, one for the apostrophe too).

  ORDER 8  -  Write the missing heading                     (Lesson 6)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    There are two blank lines between the last duty and the litany.
    Put the cursor on the first one and press o. Type
    "PAGE NINE: THE LITANY OF WARDING" and press Esc.
    (Or put the cursor on the second blank line and use O instead.)

  ORDER 9  -  Strip the heretical chant                    (Lesson 10)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Put the cursor on the "A" of the first "Ave Chaos!". Press Ctrl-v,
    then jj to cover all three lines. Press f then Space, then ; to
    stretch the block over "Ave Chaos! " (including the space).
    Press d.

  ORDER 10  -  Bring back the hidden verse          (Lessons 8, 15)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Type :split. In one window press G to jump to the bottom of the
    file and put the cursor on "From the whispers of the warp...".
    Press yy. Press Ctrl-w w to go back to the other window, put the
    cursor on "From the curse of the mutant..." and press p.
    Type :only to close the extra window.
    Do not delete the hidden copy at the bottom. It stays.


  CHECK YOUR WORK
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :w
    :!diff % %:h/keys/01-lectitio-divinitatus-solved.txt
