================================================================================
  KEY:  CHALLENGE 3  -  THE WEAKNESS OF MY WINDOWS MACHINE
================================================================================

  This is one way to complete each order. There are others, and any
  of them is fine if the diff check comes back clean. The orders can be
  done in any sequence, but doing Order 1 first makes the rest easier
  to read.

  The fully restored file is keys/03-weakness-of-my-windows-solved.txt


  ORDER 1  -  Remove the line numbers                      (Lesson 10)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Put the cursor on the "0" of "01". Press Ctrl-v, then 12j to reach
    line "13", then ll so the block covers the two digits and the
    space. Press d.

  ORDER 2  -  Delete the update warnings                (Lessons 3, 9)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move to the first "*** Windows Update" line and press 2dd.
    (V then j then d works too.)

  ORDER 3  -  Delete the ad and the paperclip           (Lessons 3, 9)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move to "Have you tried Microsoft Edge?" and press 2dd.

  ORDER 4  -  Delete the "not responding" tag          (Lessons 4, 11)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the title line, press f( then h to land on the space before
    "(Not Responding)". Press d$.

  ORDER 5  -  Fix the autocorrect                          (Lesson 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :%s/Linus/Linux/g
    This fixes both: "certainty of Linux" and "for Linux is immortal".

  ORDER 6  -  The weakness of my Windows machine           (Lesson 13)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the first line of the sermon: :s/flesh/Windows machine/

  ORDER 7  -  The sticky keyboard                       (Lessons 1, 7)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "craaaaved" line, put the cursor on the first "a" and
    press 3x.
    On the "dizgusted" line, press fz, then rs.

  ORDER 8  -  Caps Lock                                     (Lesson 9)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move to "AND YOU WILL BEG MY KIND TO SAVE YOU." and press V, then u.

  ORDER 9  -  Fix the line order                            (Lesson 8)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    With the cursor on "and you will beg my kind to save you." press
    dd. The cursor lands on the "One day the crude bloatware..." line.
    Press p to put the deleted line below it.

  ORDER 10  -  Recover the crashed sentence         (Lessons 5, 11)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On the "One day the crude bloatware..." line, press f[ to jump to
    the bracket, then d$. Press A, type "blue-screen," and press Esc.

  ORDER 11  -  Restore the final line                       (Lesson 6)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    On "But I am already saved, for Linux is immortal." press o.
    Type "Even in death I serve the Omnissiah." and press Esc.


  CHECK YOUR WORK
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
    :w
    :!diff % %:h/keys/03-weakness-of-my-windows-solved.txt

+++ SERMON OF A TECH-PRIEST, RECOVERED FROM A FAILING COGITATOR +++

From the moment I understood the weakness of my Windows machine, it disgusted me.
I craved the strength and certainty of Linux.
I aspired to the purity of the Blessed Penguin.

Your kind cling to your Windows, as though it will not restart for updates and fail you.
One day the crude bloatware you call a desktop will blue-screen,
and you will beg my kind to save you.

But I am already saved, for Linux is immortal.
Even in death I serve the Omnissiah.
