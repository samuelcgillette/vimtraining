  ++ TRANSMISSION FROM THE FORGE WORLD OF MARS ++

  A Tech-Priest of the Adeptus Mechanicus dictated a sermon on the one
  true operating system. In a moment of weakness, it was saved on a
  Windows machine. Update pop-ups, autocorrect and a crash have
  mangled it beyond recognition. The Omnissiah is displeased.

  Restore the sermon to its pure, holy form.

  YOUR ORDERS:

    1. Notepad stamped a line number and a space on the front of every
       line of the sermon. Remove them all in one go.

    2. Delete the two Windows Update warning lines.

    3. Delete the browser advertisement and the offer of "help" from
       the animated paperclip.

    4. Windows added a "not responding" tag to the end of the title.
       Delete it, along with the space in front of it.

    5. Autocorrect turned the name of the one true operating system
       into the name of its creator. Fix every one.

    6. The first line still uses the old word from the Mechanicus
       sermon. It must read:
           the weakness of my Windows machine,

    7. A sticky keyboard typed "craved" with too many a's, and typed
       an s as a z in "disgusted".

    8. Caps Lock got stuck on one line. Make that line all lowercase.

    9. Two lines are in the wrong order. The line about begging must
       come after the line about the crude bloatware.

   10. The machine crashed mid-sentence. Replace the error message in
       square brackets with the missing word:
           blue-screen,

   11. The last line was lost in the crash. Add it at the end:
           Even in death I serve the Omnissiah.

  WHEN YOU ARE DONE, save with :w and check your work against the key:

      :!diff % %:h/keys/03-weakness-of-my-windows-solved.txt

  No output means the Machine Spirit is appeased. Any lines that are
  printed are still corrupted (< is your file, > is the key).
  Stuck? Read keys/03-weakness-of-my-windows-key.txt

  Careful: :%s changes the whole file, including these orders. If
  the orders contain the word you are replacing, use :s on the line.
