  ++ PRIORITY ORDER FROM THE ADEPTUS MINISTORUM ++

  Scribe-Initiate, a cell of Chaos cultists broke into the shrine-library
  of Hive Tertius and defiled three pages of the Lectitio Divinitatus.
  The faithful cannot pray from a corrupted text. Restore it.

  YOUR ORDERS:

    1. The cultists scrawled their own blasphemies onto PAGE SEVEN.
       Delete every line that does not praise the Emperor (3 lines).

    2. Everywhere the Emperor is named, the cultists wrote the title of
       the arch-traitor Horus instead. Restore the true name throughout.

    3. Two letters on PAGE SEVEN were carved over with eight-pointed
       stars (they look like the number eight). Each should be an "e".

    4. A cultist added a mocking aside in parentheses on PAGE SEVEN.
       Delete it, along with the space in front of it.

    5. The bullet points ("- ") were scraped off the four duties on
       PAGE EIGHT. Put them back.

    6. The ending of the fourth duty was rewritten. Delete everything
       from its comma onward and write the true ending:
           , so that your soul stays pure.

    7. A daemon's name was written on PAGE EIGHT. The Inquisition
       demands it be redacted: replace each of its characters with X.

    8. The heading of PAGE NINE was torn out. Write it back into the
       gap above the litany:
           PAGE NINE: THE LITANY OF WARDING

    9. Every line of the litany was prefixed with a heretical chant.
       Remove it from all of them.

   10. The last verse of the litany was cut out. A loyal Sister hid a
       copy at the very bottom of this file. Use split view to bring it
       back as the last line of the litany.

  WHEN YOU ARE DONE, save with :w and check your work against the key:

      :!diff % %:h/keys/01-lectitio-divinitatus-solved.txt

  No output means the scripture is pure. Any lines that are printed
  are still defiled (< is your file, > is the key).
  Stuck? Read keys/01-lectitio-divinitatus-key.txt
