================================================================================
HOW TO USE THIS FILE
================================================================================

  - Move with h j k l. Enter Insert mode with i or a. Return to Normal
    mode with Esc. All commands in this file are typed in Normal mode.

  - Each lesson explains a command, then gives you 5 practice exercises.

  - Practice lines start with --->. Edit them until they match the
    "goal" line underneath. Leave the goal lines alone.

  - Made a mistake? Press u to undo it (Lesson 2 covers this).

  - Want to start over? Quit without saving with :q! and open the file
    again. If you already saved, run this in your terminal to reset it:
        git checkout vim-practice.txt

  - Save your progress at any time with :w

  - The whole file takes about 15 minutes. Scroll down with j to begin.


================================================================================
LESSON 1:  x  -  DELETE ONE CHARACTER
================================================================================

  x deletes the character under the cursor.

    1. Move the cursor onto the extra character.
    2. Press x.

  You can add a count: 3x deletes three characters in a row.

  1) ---> The Emperorr protects.
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) ---> Only in deathh does duty end.
     goal Only in death does duty end.

  3) ---> Knowwledge is power, guard it welll.
     goal Knowledge is power, guard it well.

  4) ---> Blesssed is the mind too small for doubbt.
     goal Blessed is the mind too small for doubt.

  5) ---> Innnnocence proves nothing.          (try 2x)
     goal Innocence proves nothing.

LESSON 2:  u  and  Ctrl-r  -  UNDO AND REDO
================================================================================

  u       undoes your last change. Press it again to undo the one before.
  Ctrl-r  redoes a change you just undid.

  You can undo as far back as you like, so don't be afraid to experiment.

  1) This line is already correct. Delete the "E" with x, then press u
     to bring it back.
     ---> The Emperor protects.
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) Fix the line with x. Press u to undo your fix. Press Ctrl-r to
     redo it.
     ---> Heresyy grows from idleness.
     goal Heresy grows from idleness.

  3) This line is correct. Delete the word "denies" by pressing x six
     times. Then press u six times until the line is restored.
     ---> Fear denies faith.
     goal Fear denies faith.

  4) Delete the two extra exclamation marks with x. Press u twice to
     bring them back. Press Ctrl-r twice to delete them again.
     ---> A suspicious mind is a healthy mind.!!
     goal A suspicious mind is a healthy mind.

  5) Delete "no " (three characters) with 3x. Undo it with u. Redo it
     with Ctrl-r. Then undo it one last time with u.
     ---> Victory needs no explanation, defeat allows none.
     goal Victory needs no explanation, defeat allows none.

LESSON 3:  dd  -  DELETE A WHOLE LINE
================================================================================

  dd deletes the entire line the cursor is on.

  Add a count to delete several lines at once:
    2dd  deletes this line and the one below it.
    3dd  deletes three lines, and so on.

  Delete the lines that do not belong in each record.

  1) Delete the middle line.
     ---> In the grim darkness of the far future,
     ---> the Emperor is just a guy sitting on a chair.
     ---> there is only war.

  2) Delete the middle line.
     ---> Even in death
     ---> I would honestly rather retire.
     ---> I still serve.

  3) Delete the two middle lines with 2dd.
     ---> Burn the heretic.
     ---> Hug the heretic.
     ---> Give the heretic a cookie.
     ---> Kill the mutant.

  4) Delete the three middle lines with 3dd.
     ---> The Emperor protects.
     ---> Blood for the Blood God!
     ---> Skulls for the Skull Throne!
     ---> Milk for the Khorne Flakes!
     ---> Only in death does duty end.

  5) Delete the last four lines with 4dd.
     ---> Hope is the first step on the road to disappointment.
     ---> ZOG DA HUMIES
     ---> ORKS IZ DA BEST
     ---> RED WUNZ GO FASTA
     ---> WAAAGH!

LESSON 4:  d + a motion  -  DELETE WORDS AND PARTS OF LINES
================================================================================

  d is the "delete" operator. Follow it with a motion and it deletes
  everything the motion would move over.

    dw   delete to the start of the next word
    de   delete to the end of the current word
    db   delete back to the start of the word
    d$   delete from the cursor to the end of the line

  Add a count to repeat the motion: d2w deletes two words.

  1) Put the cursor on the "t" in "truly" and press dw.
     ---> The Emperor truly protects.
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) Put the cursor on the space after "end." and press d$.
     ---> Only in death does duty end. Also on weekends.
     goal Only in death does duty end.

  3) Put the cursor on the first "very" and press d2w.
     ---> Knowledge is very very power, guard it well.
     goal Knowledge is power, guard it well.

  4) Put the cursor on the "i" in "smallish" and press de.
     ---> Blessed is the mind too smallish for doubt.
     goal Blessed is the mind too small for doubt.

  5) Put the cursor on the "n" in "nothing" and press db.
     ---> Innocence proves absolutely nothing.
     goal Innocence proves nothing.

LESSON 5:  A  -  APPEND AT THE END OF THE LINE
================================================================================

  A jumps to the end of the line and puts you in Insert mode, no matter
  where the cursor is on the line. Type your text, then press Esc.

  Finish each line. Your cursor can start anywhere on the line.

  1) ---> The Emperor
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) ---> Only in death does
     goal Only in death does duty end.

  3) ---> Knowledge is power,
     goal Knowledge is power, guard it well.

  4) ---> There is only
     goal There is only war.

  5) ---> An open mind is like a fortress
     goal An open mind is like a fortress with its gates unbarred and unguarded.

LESSON 6:  o  and  O  -  OPEN A NEW LINE
================================================================================

  o  opens a new line BELOW the cursor and puts you in Insert mode.
  O  opens a new line ABOVE the cursor and puts you in Insert mode.

  Type the missing line, then press Esc. You don't need to type the --->.

  1) Use o on the first line to add "there is only war." below it.
     ---> In the grim darkness of the far future,

  2) Use o on the first line to add "Kill the mutant." below it.
     ---> Burn the heretic.
     ---> Purge the unclean.

  3) Use O on the first line to add "Burn the heretic." above it.
     ---> Kill the mutant.
     ---> Purge the unclean.

  4) Use O to add "For the Emperor!" above this line.
     ---> Courage and honour!

  5) Use O to add "Thought for the day:" above this line.
     ---> Hope is the first step on the road to disappointment.

LESSON 8:  y  and  p  -  COPY (YANK) AND PASTE
================================================================================

  y is the "yank" (copy) operator. It works with motions just like d.

    yy   yank the whole line
    yw   yank to the start of the next word
    p    paste after the cursor (or below, for whole lines)
    P    paste before the cursor (or above, for whole lines)

  Counts work too: 2yy yanks two lines, 3p pastes three times.
  Bonus: dd also yanks the line it deletes, so dd then p moves a line.

  1) Use yy then p to make a second copy of this line.
     ---> I am Alpharius.
     goal two lines that both say "I am Alpharius."

  2) Use yy then 2p so this line appears three times in total.
     ---> For the Emperor!
     goal three lines that all say "For the Emperor!"

  3) Put the cursor on the first "E" and press yw. Then move to the "p"
     in the second "protects" and press P.
     ---> The Emperor protects. The protects.
     goal The Emperor protects. The Emperor protects.

  4) Put the cursor on the first line and press 2yy. Move down to the
     second line and press p.
     ---> Courage and honour!
     ---> For the Emperor!
     goal the two lines, then the same two lines again.

  5) These lines are in the wrong order. Use dd on the first line, then
     press p to put it below the other one.
     ---> there is only war.
     ---> In the grim darkness of the far future,
     goal "In the grim darkness..." first, "there is only war." second.

LESSON 9:  .  -  REPEAT THE LAST CHANGE
================================================================================

  . (the period key) repeats your last change. Make an edit once, then
  move somewhere else and press . to do it again.

  1) Press x on the first "!", then press . until they are all gone.
     ---> The Emperor protects.!!!!!
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) Delete the first "HERESY" line with dd. Move to the next "HERESY"
     line and press . to delete it too. Repeat for the last one.
     ---> Only in death does duty end.
     ---> HERESY
     ---> Even in death I still serve.
     ---> HERESY
     ---> Knowledge is power, guard it well.
     ---> HERESY

  3) On the first line, type A then ! then Esc. Press j, then . to add
     "!" to the next line. Do the same for the third line.
     ---> For the Emperor
     ---> For the Throne
     ---> For Terra
     goal all three lines end with "!"

  4) Put the cursor on the first "denies" and press dw. Then press .
     once to delete the next one.
     ---> Fear denies denies denies faith.
     goal Fear denies faith.

  5) Press o, type "Ave Imperator!", then press Esc. Press . twice more.
     ---> Glory to the Imperium.
     goal "Ave Imperator!" appears on three new lines below this one.

LESSON 10:  f  t  F  T  -  JUMP TO A CHARACTER ON THE LINE
================================================================================

  These commands jump to a specific character on the current line.

    f<char>   jump forward ONTO the next <char>
    t<char>   jump forward to just BEFORE the next <char>
    F<char>   jump backward ONTO the previous <char>
    T<char>   jump backward to just AFTER the previous <char>
    ;         repeat the last f, t, F or T
    ,         repeat it in the opposite direction

  Example: fz jumps to the next "z". Use x or r to fix what you land on.

  1) Start at the beginning of the line. Press fz, then x.
     ---> Victory needs no explanation, defeat allows nonez.
     goal Victory needs no explanation, defeat allows none.

  2) Start at the beginning. Press fq, then x. Then press ; and x
     to fix the next one. Repeat for the last one.
     ---> Succqess is commemoqrated; failure merqely remembered.
     goal Success is commemorated; failure merely remembered.

  3) Start at the beginning. Press t. (t then period) to land just
     before the period, then press x.
     ---> There is no such thing as innocence, only degrees of guiltt.
     goal There is no such thing as innocence, only degrees of guilt.

  4) Press $ to go to the end. Press Fu to jump back to the "u", then
     press ro to replace it.
     ---> Hupe is the first step on the road to disappointment.
     goal Hope is the first step on the road to disappointment.

  5) Press $ to go to the end. Press Th to land just after the last "h".
     Press ; to repeat it, landing just after the "h" in "deaths".
     Then press x.
     ---> The wise man learns from the deaths of others.
     goal The wise man learns from the death of others.

LESSON 11:  d + f/t  -  DELETE UP TO A CHARACTER
================================================================================

  f and t are motions, so they work with d just like w and $ do.

    dt<char>   delete up to, but not including, <char>
    df<char>   delete up to and including <char>
    dT<char>   delete backward to just after <char>
    d2f<char>  delete through the second <char> (counts work too)

  This is one of the fastest ways to delete part of a line.

  1) Put the cursor on the space after "protects" and press dt.
     ---> The Emperor protects (terms and conditions apply).
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) Put the cursor at the start of the line and press df then Space.
     ---> Erm, Knowledge is power, guard it well.
     goal Knowledge is power, guard it well.

  3) Put the cursor on the space after "mind" and press d2f)
     ---> Blessed is the mind (citation needed) (source: trust me) too small for doubt.
     goal Blessed is the mind too small for doubt.

  4) Put the cursor on the period at the end and press dTg
     ---> Innocence proves nothing whatsoever.
     goal Innocence proves nothing.

  5) Put the cursor on the space after "live" and press df)
     ---> Suffer not the alien to live (unless it is really cute).
     goal Suffer not the alien to live.

LESSON 12:  :s  -  FIND AND REPLACE
================================================================================

  :s/old/new/       replace the first "old" on the current line
  :s/old/new/g      replace every "old" on the current line
  :%s/old/new/g     replace every "old" in the whole file
  :%s/old/new/gc    same, but ask before each one (y = yes, n = no)

  Leave "new" empty to delete the text: :s/old//

  Type the command and press Enter. Your cursor must be on the line for
  the commands without %.

  1) Put the cursor on the line and type :s/Emprah/Emperor/
     ---> The Emprah protects.
     goal The Emperor protects.

  2) Put the cursor on the line and type :s/teh/the/g
     ---> Kill teh mutant. Burn teh heretic. Purge teh unclean.
     goal Kill the mutant. Burn the heretic. Purge the unclean.

  3) Fix all three lines at once with :%s/Horsu/Horus/g
     ---> Horsu was the Warmaster of the Imperium.
     ---> Horsu turned against the Emperor.
     ---> The Emperor defeated Horsu aboard the Vengeful Spirit.
     goal all three lines say "Horus" instead

  4) Only some of these need fixing. Type :%s/which/witch/gc
     Vim stops at each match. Press y only when the match comes right
     after "the" or "The". Press n for every other match.
     ---> Suffer not the which to live.
     ---> The which was found, which pleased the Inquisitor.
     goal Suffer not the witch to live.
     goal The witch was found, which pleased the Inquisitor.

  5) Delete text by leaving the replacement empty: :s/, mostly//
     ---> The Emperor protects, mostly.
     goal The Emperor protects.

LESSON 13:  :!  -  RUN SHELL COMMANDS
================================================================================

  You can run terminal commands without leaving Vim.

    :!command      run a command and show its output (press Enter after)
    :r !command    insert the command's output below the cursor
    :.!command     replace the current line with the command's output

  These work in terminal Vim on Linux, macOS and Git Bash. The VS Code
  Vim extension does not fully support them.

  1) Type :!ls and press Enter. You'll see the files in this folder.
     Press Enter again to come back.

  2) Type :!echo For the Emperor and press Enter. Press Enter again to
     come back.

  3) Put the cursor on the line below and type :r !date
     The current date appears on a new line underneath.
     ---> Date of correction:

  4) Put the cursor on the line below and type :r !whoami
     Your username appears on a new line underneath.
     ---> Corrected by:

  5) Put the cursor on the line below and type :.!tr a-z A-Z
     The line is run through the "tr" command and comes back uppercase.
     ---> for the emperor
     goal FOR THE EMPEROR

LESSON 14:  SPLIT VIEW
================================================================================

  You can view two files, or two parts of the same file, side by side.

    :split     (or :sp)  split the window top and bottom
    :vsplit    (or :vs)  split the window left and right
    Ctrl-w w             jump to the other window
    Ctrl-w h/j/k/l       jump to the window left/down/up/right
    :q                   close the window you are in
    :only                close every window except the one you are in

  The ARCHIVE at the very bottom of this file has the correct copies of
  some damaged records. Use split view to copy them up here.

  1) Type :split. You now see this file twice. Press Ctrl-w w to jump
     between the two windows. Close one with :q.

  2) Type :split. In one window, press G to go to the bottom and find
     Archive 1. Yank the quote with yy. Press Ctrl-w w to go back to the
     other window, put the cursor on the damaged line, and press p.
     Move back up to the damaged line and delete it with dd.
     ---> [RECORD DAMAGED]

  3) Do the same for Archive 2, but use :vsplit this time.
     ---> [RECORD DAMAGED]

  4) Do the same for Archive 3. Use Ctrl-w h/j/k/l to switch windows.
     ---> [RECORD DAMAGED]

  5) Archive 4 is two lines long. Use 2yy to yank both, paste them
     below the damaged line, then delete the damaged line. When you are
     done, type :only to close the extra window.
     ---> [RECORD DAMAGED]
